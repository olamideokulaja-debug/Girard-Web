WHAT VERIFIED MEANS
01

Title

The document is read, not glanced at. Where the title is defective or unregistered, the property does not go up.

02

Ownership

We confirm the person instructing us is the person entitled to instruct us, and that they can let or sell it.

03

Condition

Somebody attends and photographs the property as it is. The pictures on this page are of that property.

04

Money

Rent is collected through a licensed processor and the landlord is paid directly. Our 5% comes out of it, never on top.

HOW IT WORKS

Apply, sign and pay in one place.

Book a viewing, submit an application with your documents, sign the tenancy with a recorded signature and pay from the same screen. Nothing moves to WhatsApp and nothing depends on somebody remembering.

Repairs are reported against the property, not against a phone number. You can see what was raised, who it went to and when it was closed.
