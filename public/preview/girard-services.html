WHAT WE DO

Six lines of work.

Girard develops, manages, lets and advises on property in Lagos. The six below are separate mandates, not a menu — most clients arrive through one and stay for two.

01

Real estate development

Residential, commercial and mixed-use schemes, taken from land through planning and construction to handover, against international standards rather than local minimums.

  • Site acquisition and feasibility
  • Design and planning management
  • Construction oversight
  • Handover and snagging
02

Property & estate management

The unglamorous work that decides whether a building holds its value: facilities, leases, service charge, maintenance and reporting, monitored rather than remembered.

  • Tenancy administration and renewals
  • Service charge and collections
  • Planned and reactive maintenance
  • Owner reporting
03

Short let & holiday stays

Serviced residences positioned, priced, booked and kept, so an owner receives income without running an operation.

  • Positioning and nightly pricing
  • Booking and guest handling
  • Housekeeping and turnover
  • Compliance and safety
04

Buy-to-let solutions

Income-producing homes selected on financial modelling rather than optimism, then let and managed on the same terms as the rest of the portfolio.

  • Yield and cashflow modelling
  • Acquisition and title verification
  • Letting and tenant vetting
  • Ongoing management
05

Investment & partnerships

Development partnerships, joint ventures and land banking, under governance and reporting that stands up to scrutiny from outside the deal.

  • Structuring and documentation
  • Partner and investor reporting
  • Land banking
  • Exit planning
06

Advisory & transactions

Legal, financial and feasibility work across acquisitions, disposals and restructuring, including the cases where the honest advice is not to proceed.

  • Due diligence and title
  • Valuation and feasibility
  • Acquisition and disposal
  • Restructuring
