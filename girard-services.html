01 / DEVELOPMENT

Real estate development

Residential, commercial and mixed-use schemes planned and delivered against international standards, from site assembly through to handover.

  • Feasibility before designThe scheme is tested on numbers first. If it does not work on a spreadsheet it does not get drawn.
  • Consultants appointed properlyArchitects and engineers engaged under written terms with a phased milestone schedule, not a handshake.
  • Progress you can seeSite reports with photographs, video and dated notes, published to the people funding the work rather than described over the phone.

Current: Ascendant House, a private residence at Orange Island, Lekki Phase 1, with Design Group Nigeria Limited appointed as architect.

02 / MANAGEMENT

Property & estate management

Facilities, leases, service charge and maintenance, monitored rather than remembered. The owner does not chase anybody.

  • Rent collected through a licensed processorThe landlord is paid directly. Our 5% is taken out of the rent, never added on top of it.
  • Repairs on a recordA fault is raised against the property, routed to a vendor and closed with evidence. The trail survives whoever was on duty.
  • Service charge that reconcilesBudgets, statements and arrears held per unit, so a dispute is answered with a document.
03 / SHORT LET

Short let & holiday stays

Serviced residences positioned, booked, cleaned and kept, so the owner does none of it and the guest notices none of it.

  • Priced against the calendarNightly rates set on demand and season rather than on what the flat next door is asking.
  • Turnaround managedCleaning, linen, consumables and inspection between stays, recorded per booking.
  • Paid on bookingGuests pay in the app, funds settle through the processor, and the owner sees the earnings without asking for them.
04 / BUY-TO-LET

Buy-to-let solutions

Income-producing homes chosen on financial modelling rather than on optimism, then let and managed by the people who recommended them.

  • The yield is shown, not promisedPurchase price, void assumptions, service charge, management and tax, set out before you commit.
  • One party accountableThe firm that models the return is the firm that has to deliver it, which changes how the model is written.
  • Exit considered at entryHow the asset sells, or swaps, is part of the recommendation rather than a problem for later.
05 / INVESTMENT

Investment & partnerships

Development partnerships and land banking under real governance: defined shares, defined reporting and a defined way out.

  • Structure agreed in writing firstWho contributes what, who decides what, and what happens when a partner wants out.
  • Reporting on a scheduleCost to date, cost to complete and programme against plan, issued whether or not the news is good.
  • Land held with clean titleNothing is banked on our behalf or yours without the title work being done.
06 / ADVISORY

Advisory & transactions

Legal, financial and feasibility work across acquisitions, disposals and restructuring, led by a chief executive who practised real estate law for a decade.

  • Due diligenceTitle, encumbrance, planning and condition, reported plainly with the risks named rather than buried.
  • Transaction managementHeads of terms through to completion, with one party holding the timetable.
  • RestructuringHolding structures, joint ventures and portfolio reorganisation, including the tax and governance consequences.
