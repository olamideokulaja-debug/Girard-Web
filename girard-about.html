ABOUT GIRARD

A property firm
that keeps records.

Girard Property Limited develops, verifies and manages property across Lagos, and runs the platform the work is recorded in.

Lagos does not have a shortage of listings. It has a shortage of listings that are real.

The same flat appears on 6 platforms at 4 prices. An agent takes an inspection fee for a property that was let a month ago. A tenant pays a deposit into a personal account and has nothing on paper when it goes wrong. None of that is a technology problem in the first instance. It is a problem of who checked, and whether anybody wrote it down.

Girard was built the other way round. Title, ownership and condition are checked before a property is published. Rent is collected through a licensed processor and paid to the landlord directly. Tenancies are signed with a recorded, attributed signature that cannot be altered afterwards. Repairs are raised against the property and closed with evidence.

The result is slower than the alternative, and the listing count is smaller. That is the trade, and we would make it again.
